# Interview script (M0) — 5 people, ~10 minutes each

Talk to 5 of the people who sent photos. Show them the answer the AI gave for **their**
item (screenshot or read it out). Don't pitch; listen. Write short notes.

1. **Last time:** "Tell me about the last time something broke or went missing and you
   needed a replacement. What did you do? How long did it take? Did you find it?"
2. **Today's way:** "How would you normally find this part? (Shop, Amazon/Flipkart,
   Google, service centre, calling someone, gave up?)"
3. **Our answer:** show it. "Is this right? Is anything confusing? What would you do next
   after seeing this?"
4. **Trust:** "It says *'Possible — not confirmed'* here. Does that make you trust it more
   or less? What would make you confident enough to buy?"
5. **Extra photo:** "It asked for a photo of the bottom / a measurement. Would you do that?"
6. **Frequency:** "How often does something like this happen in your house? (per month /
   per year)"
7. **Alternatives:** "Have you tried Google Lens or ChatGPT for this kind of thing? How was
   it?"
8. **Value:** "If an app did this well, would you keep it on your phone? Would you pay
   ₹99 / ₹199 a month, or rather ₹49 for 10 searches when you need it — or neither?"

Record per person: frequency, current method, trust reaction, payment answer, and one quote.
