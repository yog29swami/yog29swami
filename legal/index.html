Privacy Policy

TryOn Studio ("the app") lets you preview glasses, earrings and necklaces on a photo of yourself. This policy explains what happens to your photos and data when you use it.

Effective 7 Sep 2026 v1.0
📵

Your photo never leaves your device. Face detection runs entirely on-device using Google's ML Kit. We do not have servers that store, view, or process your camera roll or try-on photos.

1 What we collect

The app is designed to work without an account and without sending your photos anywhere. Here is exactly what touches your data, and where it goes:

DataPurposeLeaves your device?
Camera preview / selected photoDetect your face and draw the try-on overlayNo — processed on-device
Saved try-on photoOnly written to your photo library when you tap "Save"No
Favorites & subscription statusRemember your picks between sessionsNo — stored locally on your device
Advertising identifierShow ads to free-tier users, via Google AdMobYes — to Google, see §2
Purchase/receipt dataValidate and manage your Pro subscription, via RevenueCatYes — to RevenueCat, see §2

2 Third-party services

Two service providers process limited data on our behalf:

  • Google AdMob shows banner ads to users on the free tier and may use your device's advertising identifier to do so. You can reset or limit this identifier in your device's ad settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat manages our Pro subscription and receives purchase receipts from the App Store or Google Play to confirm your subscription status. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Neither service ever receives your camera roll, your try-on photos, or the products you tried on.

3 Permissions we ask for

  • Camera — to show you a live preview and take a try-on photo.
  • Photo library (read) — only if you choose "Choose Photo" instead of the camera.
  • Photo library (write) — only when you tap "Save" on a finished try-on.

You can revoke any of these at any time in your device settings; the app will simply ask again next time you use the related feature.

4 Children's privacy

TryOn Studio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us through third-party services described above, contact us using §7 and we will act on it.

5 Your choices

Because your photos stay on your device, deleting them is entirely in your hands — remove them from your photo library or uninstall the app to clear locally stored favorites and settings. To manage ad personalization or your subscription, use your device's or app store account settings.

6 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
